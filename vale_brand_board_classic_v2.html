08

Do and don't

✓ Do

  • Give every heading exactly one Cormorant italic word
  • Keep sage and birch to thin lines and line icons
  • Let white space carry the premium feel, Skargards style
  • Use one dark band at most, spaced well away from the footer

✗ Don't

  • Fill large areas with sage or birch
  • Use bold headings. Weight here is regular, the italic word does the lifting
  • Round the card corners. 6px is the look
  • Mix in BSUK honey, forest green or Raleway
