Doodles and icons

07

Photo style

Calm, warm, lived in. The tub is the hero and it is always shown whole.

Golden hour or dusk

Warm evening light, steam, string lights or a lit stove. Avoid flat midday shots.

Relaxed, real people

Someone soaking, a mug on the rim, a towel over the edge. Never posed or showy.

The whole tub, uncropped

Shown at its real shape on its real ground: deck, gravel, garden. Light or neutral surroundings.

  • No BSUK branding, mats or barrel saunas in a Vale photo.
  • Liam signs off every AI-touched image before it goes on the stand or the site.
08

Do and don't

✓ Do

  • Lead every heading with a thin phrase and land it on a heavy one
  • Leave lots of grey space around white cards
  • Keep copy short and slow: lid off, lights on, nowhere to be
  • Use one doodle to add warmth where a page feels cold

✗ Don't

  • Add gold, navy, sage or wood tones. This edition has no accent colour
  • Use Cormorant anywhere but the wordmark
  • Put dark bands back to back, or more than one dark band per piece
  • Mix in BSUK honey, forest green or Raleway
